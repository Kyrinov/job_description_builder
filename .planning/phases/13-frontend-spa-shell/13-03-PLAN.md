## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-13-03-01 | Tampering | localStorage.getItem('jd-builder-v2-record') | mitigate | Lazy initializer wraps JSON.parse in try/catch; malformed JSON falls back to `{}` without throwing. Corrupted record causes a fresh start — acceptable for local single-user crash-recovery. |
| T-13-03-02 | Denial of Service | localStorage.setItem quota exceeded | mitigate | useEffect wraps setItem in try/catch; quota errors are silently swallowed (RESEARCH.md Pitfall 4). App continues working without persistence rather than crashing. |
| T-13-03-03 | Information Disclosure | record serialized to localStorage | accept | Single-user local app; localStorage is origin-scoped; no sensitive PII in the record object (position titles, branch names — internal HR data already on the user's machine). |
</threat_model>
