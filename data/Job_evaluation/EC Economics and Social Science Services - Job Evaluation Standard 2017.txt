ECONOMICS AND SOCIAL SCIENCE SERVICES GROUP JOB EVALUATION STANDARD
================================================================================

GROUP DEFINITION:
The EC Group comprises positions primarily involved in the conduct of surveys, studies and projects in the social sciences; the identification, description and organization of archival, library, museum and gallery materials; the editing of legislation or the provision of advice on legal problems in specific fields; and the application of a comprehensive knowledge of economics, sociology or statistics to the conduct of economic, socio-economic and sociological research, studies, forecasts and surveys.

TYPE OF STANDARD: Point-rating plan

NINE ELEMENTS:
1. Decision making (Responsibility) - 21% - max 210 pts
2. Leadership and operational management (Responsibility) - 14% - max 140 pts
3. Communication (Skill) - 18% - max 180 pts
4. Knowledge of specialized fields (Skill) - 10.5% - max 105 pts
5. Contextual knowledge (Skill) - 10.5% - max 105 pts
6. Research and analysis (Skill) - 21% - max 210 pts
7. Physical effort (Effort) - 1.5% - max 15 pts
8. Sensory effort (Effort) - 1.0% - max 10 pts
9. Working conditions (Working conditions) - 2.5% - max 25 pts
TOTAL: 100% - 1,000 pts

ELEMENT DEFINITIONS:

ELEMENT 1 - DECISION MAKING (RESPONSIBILITY):
Measures latitude applied and impact of decision making. Latitude measures scope to make decisions and risks involved; impact measures effect of decisions on organizations, cases, policies, geographic areas, etc.
- D1 (5 pts): Issue-specific, impact on own work unit; select and apply established guidelines; risks negligible.
- D2 (15 pts): Issue-specific, impact on components of project/study/survey; modify methods within existing precedents; risks minor and apparent.
- D3 (35 pts): Multiple issues, impact on specific projects/studies; adapt precedent; risks significant and apparent.
- D4 (60 pts): Specific subject matter areas/sectors/segments/geographies; resolve issues lacking precedent within established objectives; risks significant and not readily apparent.
- D5 (90 pts): Multiple subject matter areas/sectors/segments/geographies; resolve issues within established programs; risks significant and not readily apparent.
- D6 (125 pts): Multiple areas, impact on departmental programs or policies; modify or devise new courses of action; risks often difficult to determine.
- D7 (165 pts): Multiple areas across programs, impact on multiple government programs or policy areas; develop solutions to interrelated sensitive issues; risks uncertain and require significant risk analysis.
- D8 (210 pts): Multiple areas across levels of government, impact on direction of government policies; determine objectives for diverse and poorly defined issues; risks uncertain and require significant risk analysis.

ELEMENT 2 - LEADERSHIP AND OPERATIONAL MANAGEMENT (RESPONSIBILITY):
Measures responsibility for leading, planning and being accountable for human, financial and materiel resources to achieve work objectives.
- D1 (5 pts): Assist colleagues; explain work; team member; occasional ad hoc team lead; ensure confidentiality; maintain materiel resources.
- D2 (20 pts): Assign work; explain responsibilities; provide guidance/coaching; provide training; monitor and report on projects; recommend project team members; oversee contractors.
- D3 (50 pts): Assign work; review and provide advice; monitor work in progress; assess work unit or project results; evaluate employee performance; develop work plans; recommend human/financial/materiel resources; most performance issues handled directly.
- D4 (90 pts): Provide functional direction; manage subordinate supervisors/managers; establish priorities and work plans; manage operating budget; select project team members; leading recruitment; taking disciplinary action; authorize contracts.
- D5 (140 pts): Ensure program/project objectives achieved; report on results; establish broad priorities and division of responsibilities; select, approve and allocate human, materiel and financial resources; long-term resource planning; approve budget submissions; negotiate contractual agreements.

ELEMENT 3 - COMMUNICATION (SKILL):
Measures skills to express and receive information orally and in writing, scope of communication, and reasons why work requires contacts.
- D1 (5 pts): Convey or obtain information for collaborative working relationships and day-to-day activities.
- D2 (25 pts): Exchange specialized information OR share expertise to meet defined objectives.
- D3 (50 pts): Provide interpretation of specialized information to improve understanding.
- D4 (75 pts): Adapt information for audiences NOT familiar with specialized terminology.
- D5 (100 pts): Adapt complex information for audiences NOT familiar with specialized terminology; facilitate cooperation and consensus.
- D6 (140 pts): Defend positions and advance arguments to promote organizational or mutual business interests; influence approaches on sensitive issues.
- D7 (180 pts): Persuade where there are significant challenges for acceptance; resolve strategic issues on highly complex, critical, contentious or high profile situations.

ELEMENT 4 - KNOWLEDGE OF SPECIALIZED FIELDS (SKILL):
Measures required depth and breadth of knowledge of specialized fields (economics, sociology, statistics, other social sciences, archival/library/museum work, legal fields). Can be acquired through formal education, experience, specialized or on-the-job training.
- D1 (5 pts): Basic knowledge to accomplish defined activities by applying methods, techniques and established practices.
- D2 (15 pts): General knowledge of methods, techniques and practices AND basic knowledge of theories and principles and their relevance.
- D3 (35 pts): In-depth knowledge of methods, techniques and practices AND general knowledge of theories and principles.
- D4 (55 pts): Advanced knowledge of specialized methodologies with understanding of underlying concepts OR in-depth knowledge of theories and principles.
- D5 (80 pts): Advanced knowledge of theories or principles of a number of specialized fields and their interrelationships.
- D6 (105 pts): Expert knowledge of underlying theoretical concepts of a specialized field and of complex interrelationships with other fields.

ELEMENT 5 - CONTEXTUAL KNOWLEDGE (SKILL):
Measures required depth and breadth of contextual knowledge (subject matter, communities, organizations, networks, external environments, policies, legislation, regulations, management).
- D1 (5 pts): Basic knowledge of specific subject matter area(s); awareness of work-related legislation/policy.
- D2 (20 pts): General knowledge of specific subject matter area(s); knowledge of aspects of work-related legislation/policy.
- D3 (40 pts): General knowledge of broad subject matter area(s); knowledge of departmental business lines; knowledge of legislation/policy related to mandate and objectives.
- D4 (60 pts): In-depth knowledge of subject matter area(s); knowledge of subject matter interrelationships; knowledge of related business lines of other departments and private sectors.
- D5 (80 pts): Advanced knowledge of subject matter area(s); knowledge of implications for other subject matter areas and interrelationships across multiple programs, other levels of government, and/or external sectors.
- D6 (105 pts): Extensive knowledge of subject matter area(s); knowledge of domestic or international environments at strategic level; knowledge of government plans, priorities and directions; knowledge of legislation, policies and programs related to varied and complex business contexts.

ELEMENT 6 - RESEARCH AND ANALYSIS (SKILL):
Measures requirement to research and analyze both qualitative and quantitative information.
- D1 (5 pts): Conduct information searches, organize, review and process information; perform directed, routine analysis; ensure accuracy and completeness.
- D2 (30 pts): Determine and select information relevant to particular area of study; compile and verify information; perform directed, non-routine analyses; ensure consistency, coherence and information integrity.
- D3 (75 pts): Study range of information and identify/interpret trends and patterns; determine gaps in research; develop comprehensive background material for further analysis. OR assess range of information and evaluate/compare current approaches and practices; identify possible modifications or alternative approaches.
- D4 (120 pts): Synthesize and interpret complex information from diverse sources; identify conceptual frameworks related to area of study; account for subtle interrelationships among variables OR determine need for new analytical methodologies.
- D5 (165 pts): Investigate and challenge existing conceptual frameworks; examine multiple variables from distinct sources within a subject matter area; reach conclusions and/or generate solutions.
- D6 (210 pts): Integrate distinct broad-based qualitative or quantitative analyses; create/adapt conceptual frameworks for development, application, interpretation, and evaluation of studies, methodologies, strategies and policies.

ELEMENT 7 - PHYSICAL EFFORT (EFFORT):
Measures intensity of physical effort and frequency required. "Regular requirement of work" means duties performed as integral part of work.
- D1 (3 pts): Regularly keyboarding or performing other activities requiring small muscle movement.
- D2 (4 pts): Occasionally standing, walking, bending, reaching or lifting/moving items up to 10 kg.
- D3 (6 pts): Regularly standing, walking, bending, reaching or lifting/moving items up to 10 kg.
- D4 (10 pts): Occasionally carrying, lifting or moving items over 10 kg.
- D5 (15 pts): Regularly carrying, lifting or moving items over 10 kg.

ELEMENT 8 - SENSORY EFFORT (EFFORT):
Measures intensity, duration and frequency of sensory effort associated with exclusive use of one or more senses.
- D1 (2 pts): Occasionally using senses to detect easily perceived differences (e.g., detecting physical differences between documents, data or things).
- D2 (3 pts): Regularly using senses to detect easily perceived differences.
- D3 (5 pts): Occasionally using senses to make fine distinctions or detect barely perceptible variations.
- D4 (10 pts): Regularly using senses to make fine distinctions or detect barely perceptible variations.

ELEMENT 9 - WORKING CONDITIONS (WORKING CONDITIONS):
Measures physical and psychological surroundings and the extent to which they make the job unpleasant. Classify as: Somewhat Unpleasant / Unpleasant / Very Unpleasant.

Examples:
Somewhat Unpleasant: intra-regional travel, travel between buildings, monotony and repetition, complaints/public criticism, changing deadlines/time pressures, lack of privacy, office noise, glare from computer screen.
Unpleasant: travel to remote communities, unrelenting monotony, constant complaints, constantly changing deadlines, working in confined spaces, interactions with angry/confrontational individuals, exposure to smelly odours or fumes, exposure to dirt and dust.
Very Unpleasant: travel to remote communities during sustained inclement conditions, interactions with abusive individuals, noxious odours or fumes, filth and fungus, separation from family for lengthy periods, potential threat to personal security, potential exposure to serious infectious diseases, exposure to disturbing images or issues.

- D1 (5 pts): Exposure to somewhat unpleasant conditions.
- D2 (8 pts): Regular exposure to somewhat unpleasant conditions AND some exposure to unpleasant or very unpleasant conditions.
- D3 (12 pts): Regular exposure to unpleasant conditions.
- D4 (17 pts): Regular exposure to unpleasant conditions AND some exposure to very unpleasant conditions.
- D5 (25 pts): Regular exposure to very unpleasant conditions.
