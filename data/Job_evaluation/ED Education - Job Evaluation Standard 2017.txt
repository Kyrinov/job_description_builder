EDUCATION JOB EVALUATION STANDARD
================================================================================

This standard applies to the Education (ED) classification of the Education and Library Science (EB) Group.

GROUP DEFINITION:
The Education (ED) classification of the EB Group comprises positions primarily involved in the application of a comprehensive knowledge of educational techniques to the teaching and counselling of students in schools and to the education, training and counselling of youths and adults in out-of-school programs, to the conduct of research and to the provision of advice related to education.

THREE SUBGROUPS:
1. Language Teaching (ED-LAT) - uses level descriptions for teachers and immediate supervisors
2. Elementary and Secondary Teaching (ED-EST) - uses level descriptions for teachers and immediate supervisors
3. Education Services (ED-EDS) - uses point-rating system for specialists, advisers, consultants, and administrators

NOTE: The first two subgroups classify based on academic training and experience for basic pay; an allowance is added for administrative and supervisory responsibility.

================================================================================
SUBGROUP 1: LANGUAGE TEACHING (ED-LAT)
================================================================================

SUBGROUP DEFINITION:
Teaching or supervision of teaching of an official or foreign language to members of the Public Service of Canada and such other authorized persons.

INCLUSIONS:
- Teaching of English, French or a foreign language to public servants.
- Provision of guidance and direction to language teachers by a senior teacher.
- Administration by a school principal of a language school of the Public Service of Canada.

EXCLUSIONS:
- Teaching of elementary, secondary or vocational curricula in federal schools.
- Teaching of basic or literacy education in Aboriginal and northern communities.
- Direction of an education program or part of a program.
- Planning, development or evaluation of education programs.
- Conduct of education research, development of curricula or tests, or provision of advice.
- Planning, teaching, direction or guidance of education programs for adults other than those in language schools of the Public Service of Canada.

LEVEL DESCRIPTIONS:
Level 1 - Teacher: Under general supervision. Teaches a second language (English, French, or foreign language) by developing lesson plans and classroom exercises; instructing students using selected teaching aids; giving remedial instruction as necessary. Tests students for proficiency. Keeps informed of developments in language-teaching field.

Level 2 - Senior Teacher: Under direction. Fosters development of teaching skills in language teachers by reviewing and criticizing work in classroom; discussing problematic areas; recommending modification of course content; assessing student response and performance; reviewing and comparing test results. Assigns teachers to classes. Occasionally teaches to demonstrate methodology.

Level 3 - Principal: Under direction. Directs, evaluates and guides the work of senior teachers and teachers. Provides school facilities by allocating space, procuring equipment and teaching materials, arranging teaching timetables. Inspects school facilities. May direct extra-curricular activities or housekeeping services.

================================================================================
SUBGROUP 2: ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY TEACHING (ED-EST)
================================================================================

SUBGROUP DEFINITION:
Teaching and counselling of students in elementary and secondary schools; teaching and counselling of youths and adults; supervision of teaching and counselling activities.

INCLUSIONS:
- Teaching of elementary, secondary or vocational curricula in federal schools.
- Teaching of classes in literacy and adult education.
- Counselling of students.
- Supervision of any of the above duties as department heads, assistant principals and principals.

LEVEL DESCRIPTIONS:
Level 1 - Teacher or Equivalent: Under general supervision. Teaches academic, technical, vocational, or adult education subjects. Tests students. Keeps informed of developments. OR Guides and counsels students through individual or group interviews, arranging diagnostic services, discussing behavioral problems, providing vocational information.

Level 2 - Department Head or Equivalent: Under direction. Plans, implements and supervises teaching of a particular area or subject in accordance with the approved curriculum. Advises teachers on methodology; selects appropriate resources; evaluates and revises instructional methods and materials.

Level 3 - Assistant Principal or Equivalent: Under direction. Assists principal with administration of a school. Allocates classrooms and equipment; disciplines students; performs duties assigned by principal.

Level 4 - Principal or Equivalent: Under direction. Administers academic or vocational program and supporting services. Supervises classroom instruction; evaluates the academic/vocational program; reviews student achievement; meets with teaching staff; supervises teacher/student assignment to classes; inspects school facilities; requests supplies and equipment.

================================================================================
SUBGROUP 3: EDUCATION SERVICES (ED-EDS) - POINT RATING PLAN
================================================================================

SUBGROUP DEFINITION:
Planning, development, direction or evaluation of education programs (language teaching, vocational training, elementary and secondary teaching, adult education, literacy education, health education); conduct of educational research; provision of advice.

INCLUSIONS:
- Conduct of education research (curriculum or test research and development).
- Evaluation of course or program content; planning and development of new programs.
- Evaluation of teaching methodology and teacher performance.
- Direction of an education program, or part of a program.
- Provision of advice on any of the aforementioned duties.
- Supervision or direction of any of these duties.

FACTORS AND ELEMENTS:
1. Knowledge (35% weight, max 350 pts)
2. Problem Solving (35% weight, max 350 pts) - two dimensional: Scope for Initiative and Imagination; Impact of Activities
3. Responsibility for Contacts (15% weight, max 150 pts) - two dimensional: Purpose and Nature of Contacts; Persons Contacted
4. Supervision (15% weight, max 150 pts) - two dimensional: Number of Employees Supervised; Level of Employees Supervised

KNOWLEDGE FACTOR - 7 degrees (70 to 350 pts):
- D1 (70 pts): Good knowledge of adult education theory and practice, or equivalent; experience in education or community development.
- D2 (116 pts): Professional knowledge of dietetics with specialization in public health/community nutrition; knowledge of socio-economic conditions.
- D3 (162 pts): Knowledge of test development, language learning, curriculum; sound understanding of research procedures.
- D4 (209 pts): Knowledge of education administration/supervision; curriculum planning; thorough knowledge of specific regional programs.
- D5 (256 pts): Thorough knowledge of second language teaching program development; knowledge of research procedures and teaching techniques.
- D6 (303 pts): Thorough knowledge of vocational training, adult education, programs; knowledge of relevant legislation and regulations.
- D7 (350 pts): Broad knowledge of all aspects of educational theory and practice; thorough knowledge of relevant acts, policies, and of social, economic and cultural characteristics.

PROBLEM SOLVING FACTOR (degree grid - Scope A-C x Impact 1-3):
- Scope A: Selection from known techniques with moderate modification.
- Scope B: Substantial modification or adaptation; scope for new techniques and changes.
- Scope C: Typically no precedents exist; high degree of imagination and judgment; solutions frequently influence program content or policy objectives.
- Impact 1: Decisions affect individuals, groups or communities in a specific area.
- Impact 2: Decisions affect policies and programs for a region or substantial program area.
- Impact 3: Decisions affect national programs, policies, and major resources.

RESPONSIBILITY FOR CONTACTS FACTOR (degree grid - Purpose A-D x Persons 1-3):
Nature of Contacts:
- A: To give, obtain and exchange information requiring discussion, explanation and cooperation.
- B: To persuade and obtain assistance or agreement from others.
- C: To act as representative at formal meetings where differences in interest may be expected; authority to discuss problems and seek common ground.
- D: To act as representative in negotiating agreements of considerable significance; authority to formulate programs within established objectives.

Persons Contacted:
- 1: School children, parents, adult students, associates and colleagues in other departments, employees in same department.
- 2: Officials in other departments and agencies, and associates in other levels of government, other countries, universities and colleges, research centres, private organizations or industry.
- 3: Officials of other levels of government, other countries, universities and colleges, research centres, private organizations or industry.

SUPERVISION FACTOR (7 size categories A-G x 3 nature degrees 1-3):
- Degree 1: Continuing and substantive supervision of employees in any category.
- Degree 2: Continuing and substantive supervision of employees, one or more of whom occupy positions at levels ED-EDS-1, 2 and 3 or equivalent.
- Degree 3: Continuing and substantive supervision of employees, one or more of whom occupy positions at levels ED-EDS-4, 5, 6 and 7 or equivalent.

Number ranges: A=1-3; B=4-10; C=11-25; D=26-75; E=76-200; F=201-400; G=401+
